## The five-piece rule

1. **A corset or a corset belt** — it sets the silhouette, and it is the piece that makes the rest look deliberate.
2. **One styled layer** — a tee, a hoodie or a tailcoat, never two.
3. **Goggles** — on the hat, around the neck or pushed up; they are the signal.
4. **One brass accessory** — a pocket watch, a pendant, the buttons on a coat. One, not five.
5. **Shoes you can walk in** — boots beat heels at every convention we have been to.
