**H1:** Steampunk Outfit Ideas for Women: 12 Complete Looks

A steampunk outfit is not a costume you put on; it is four or five decisions in a fixed order. Get the order
wrong — hat first, corset last — and the pieces fight each other. Get it right and the same five pieces make a
Victorian duchess on Saturday and something you can wear to work on Tuesday.

This page is the honest version of that: twelve complete looks, each built from pieces we actually sell right
now, each with the real price in kroner and a link to the product. Nothing here is styled around a product we
have discontinued, and nothing is priced in a currency the shop does not use.

**Quick answer:** the cheapest complete look in this guide is **500,00 kr.** (a hoodie and the copper goggles),
and the most expensive is **1.465,00 kr.** (the brass pocket watch, the brocade tailcoat and the top hat). The
look most people should start with is Look 1, the Airship Captain, at **1.170,00 kr.** — it is the one that reads
as steampunk from ten metres away, and every other look on this page is a variation of its logic.

### The five-piece rule

Every look below is an instance of the same five layers. Learn the rule and you can build a sixth look from
whatever is in your wardrobe.

1. **A corset or a structured top.** It sets the silhouette, so it is chosen first and everything else is chosen
   to match it. Ours are the [Steampunk Overbust Corset — Leather Lace-Up](https://steamgears.eu/go/489/) at
   **400,00 kr.** and the [Gothic Overbust Corset — Faux Leather, Silver](https://steamgears.eu/go/491/) at
   **435,00 kr.**
2. **One outer layer with a story.** A tailcoat, a frock coat or a hoodie. The
   [Wine Red Velvet Tailcoat — Victorian Frock Coat](https://steamgears.eu/go/493/) is **435,00 kr.** and the
   [Victorian Vampire Tailcoat — Brocade](https://steamgears.eu/go/495/) is **265,00 kr.** — the brocade is the
   cheaper one and the one that photographs better under warm light.
3. **Goggles, worn somewhere unexpected.** Ours are the
   [Copper Pilot Goggles — Vintage Windproof](https://steamgears.eu/go/477/) at **165,00 kr.** On the hat, around
   the neck, on a bag strap. Never over the eyes unless you are actually at a con.
4. **One brass object.** A pocket watch, a pendant, a hat. The
   [Time Traveler Top Hat with Goggles](https://steamgears.eu/go/483/) at **335,00 kr.** does the job of two
   layers at once, which is why it appears in seven of the twelve looks.
5. **Shoes you can walk in.** Boots beat heels, always. This is the one part we do not sell, so it is also the
   part where most outfits are won or lost.

### FAQ

**How much does a complete steampunk outfit for women cost?**
Between **500,00 kr.** for the hoodie-and-goggles look and **1.465,00 kr.** for the jewellery-led one, at the
prices in our own shop. Most people land on Look 1 or Look 4, which cost 1.170,00 kr. and 580,00 kr.

**Can I wear steampunk without a corset?**
Yes. Looks 5, 8 and 10 are built without one, and Look 8 is the one we recommend if you want to wear the outfit
to work. The hat and the goggles carry the genre on their own.

**What should I buy first if I only have 500,00 kr.?**
The goggles at **165,00 kr.** and one hoodie at **335,00 kr.** — Look 5. Those two pieces work with clothes you
already own, and the goggles move to every other look on this page.

**Do these outfits work for plus-size women?**
Yes, and the lace-up construction is the reason: the two corsets are adjustable across several sizes, and the
hoodies and tees are cut unisex. Read the size chart on each product page rather than ordering your usual size.

**How do I stop the outfit looking like a costume?**
Keep it to three steampunk elements and one palette — brown, black, wine and brass. One brass object reads as a
style choice; four read as a fancy-dress outfit. Look 4 is the proof: tee, corset, boots, nothing else.
