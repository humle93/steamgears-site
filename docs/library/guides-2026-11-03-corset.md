### Copy

**H1:** Steampunk Corset: How to Choose, Style and Wear One

A steampunk corset is not one garment with three prices. It is a silhouette decision, and choosing the wrong
one is why most first corsets end up in a drawer after one evening. This guide is the one our brief asked for:
how to pick by structure, what to put under it, what to put over it, and what the two corsets in our shop are
actually for.

**Quick answer:** choose the leather overbust (**400,00 kr.**) if the corset is meant to be the outfit rather
than a layer; the silver faux leather (**435,00 kr.**) if you want hardware and an evening look. Everything
else — goggles, hat, tailcoat — is chosen to match that decision, not the other way round.

### 1. Choose by structure, not by size

**In one line:** the corset's job is to change the line of the body, so the structure decides the outfit before
the colour does.

- [Steampunk Overbust Corset — Leather Lace-Up](https://steamgears.eu/go/489/) — **400,00 kr.** Structured
  overbust in leather. It is the piece that reads as *character* in a photograph, and the one that limits what
  you can wear over it.
- [Gothic Overbust Corset — Faux Leather, Silver](https://steamgears.eu/go/491/) — **435,00 kr.** Hardware and a
  silver tone. The most evening of the two, and the one that pairs with the brass goggles rather than
  fighting them.

**What we used:** 489 for daytime looks, 491 when the outfit has metal elsewhere. Both are sized by the chart
on their own product page — read that chart rather than ordering your usual clothing size, because a corset is
fitted to the ribcage and the waist, not to the shoulder. Between two sizes, take the larger: lacing can
tighten, it cannot create room.

### 2. What goes under it

**In one line:** a cotton layer under the corset is the difference between a costume you can wear for six hours
and one you take off after forty minutes.

Under 489: the [Airship Armada — Vintage Engraving Tee](https://steamgears.eu/go/557/) at **180,00 kr.** or a
thin shirt you already own. Under 491: a camisole or a fitted top, not a loose shirt — bulk under a structured
overbust is what makes lacing uneven.

**What we used:** the engraved tee under the leather overbust, a plain black fitted top under the silver one.
Nothing under a corset should have seams running across the waistline.

### FAQ

**How do I know which corset size to order?**
Use the size chart on the product page, not your usual clothing size. A corset is fitted to the ribcage and the
waist; if you are between two sizes on the overbust, take the larger one — the lacing can tighten, it cannot
create room.

**Can I wear a steampunk corset all day?**
The leather overbust is the more forgiving of the two and works over a tee for a long day. The silver one is an
evening piece — fine for six hours, not for a working day.

**What do I wear underneath?**
A thin cotton layer: an engraved tee under the leather overbust, a fitted camisole under the silver one. Avoid
seams across the waist and avoid loose fabric, which bunches under lacing.

**Does a corset work without a coat?**
It can, but the tailcoat is what makes the outfit read as clothing rather than as underwear. If you only buy
two things, buy a corset and the brocade tailcoat.

**Can I wear the silver corset with brass goggles?**
Yes — mixed metals are period-correct for a workshop look and less so for a formal one. Match the hat to the
corset's metal if you want the outfit to look deliberate.

**What is the cheapest complete look here?**
580,00 kr.: the leather overbust at 400,00 kr. and the engraved tee at 180,00 kr. Goggles at 165,00 kr. make it
745,00 kr. and turn it into a costume.

**Why are the prices in kroner?**
Because the shop prices in DKK. Every amount here was read in the store on 9 November 2026; if a price has
moved since, the product page wins.

**Which guide should I read next?**
The [women's steampunk essentials](https://steamgears.eu/womens-steampunk-essentials/) covers the same pieces in
a ten-item wardrobe, and the [steampunk costumes hub](https://steamgears.eu/steampunk-costumes/) has the
character-by-character versions.

**How do returns work?**
EU consumers have 14 days to withdraw from the purchase. Check the return page before ordering if the fit
matters — and it does with a corset.

  kravet om mindst ét `/go/<id>/`-link, og en guide uden købsvej er den slags trafik S1.5 kalder en lækage.

  kvalitetsgaten i pluginnet selv ville flagge.
