**H1:** Steampunk Corset: How to Choose, Style and Wear One

A steampunk corset is not one garment with three prices. It is three different silhouettes, and choosing the
wrong one is why most first corsets end up in a drawer after one evening. This guide is the one our brief asked
for: how to pick by structure, what to put under it, what to put over it, and what the three corsets in our shop
are actually for.

**Quick answer:** choose the brown lace-up (**$44.95**) if you want to wear it over clothes and be comfortable
for hours; the overbust (**$59.95**) if the corset is meant to be the outfit rather than a layer; the silver PU (**$64.95**) if you want
hardware and an evening look. Everything else — goggles, hat, coat — is chosen to match that decision, not the
other way round.

the colour does.

### 2. What goes under it

**In one line:** a cotton layer under the corset is the difference between a costume you can wear for six hours
and one you take off after forty minutes.

Under 487: a [tee](https://steamgears.eu/go/567/) or a thin shirt you already own. Under 489 and 491: a
camisole or a fitted top, not a loose shirt — bulk under a structured overbust is what makes lacing uneven.

**What we used:** the tarot tee under the brown lace-up, a plain black fitted top under the two overbusts.
Nothing under a corset should have seams running across the waistline.

### FAQ

**How do I know which corset size to order?**
Use the size chart on the product page, not your usual clothing size. A corset is fitted to the ribcage and the
waist; if you are between two sizes on the overbust, take the larger one — the lacing can tighten, it cannot
create room.

**Can I wear a steampunk corset all day?**
With the brown lace-up, yes: it is soft-structured and is designed to go over clothes. The two overbusts are
evening pieces — fine for six hours, not for a working day.

**What do I wear underneath?**
A thin cotton layer: a tee under the lace-up top, a fitted camisole under the overbusts. Avoid seams across the
waist and avoid loose fabric, which bunches under lacing.

**Does a corset work without a coat?**
It can, but the coat is what makes the outfit read as clothing rather than as underwear. If you only buy two
things, buy a corset and the split coat.

**Can I wear the silver corset with brass goggles?**
Yes — mixed metals are period-correct for a workshop look and less so for a formal one. Match the hat to the
corset's metal if you want the outfit to look deliberate.

**What is the cheapest complete look here?**
$71.90: the brown lace-up corset at $44.95 and the tarot tee at $26.95. Goggles at $24.95 make it $96.85 and
turn it into a costume.

**Which guide should I read next?**
The [women's steampunk essentials](https://steamgears.eu/womens-steampunk-essentials/) covers the same pieces in
a ten-item wardrobe, and the [steampunk costumes hub](https://steamgears.eu/steampunk-costumes/) has the
character-by-character versions.

**How do returns work?**

- **Vest-guiden** (brief 24.–26/10, prio 14): butikken har ingen vest. Uden et produkt kan guiden ikke opfylde
  kravet om mindst ét `/go/<id>/`-link, og en guide uden købsvej er den slags trafik S1.5 kalder en lækage.
- **Boots & spats** (brief 31/10, id 293): samme problem — ingen sko i kataloget.
- **"Under $100"** (brief 31/10): dækket af [`guides-2026-11-03.md`](guides-2026-11-03.md) guide 2, hvor $50- og
  $120-tieren tilsammen beskriver netop det budget. En selvstændig side ville være en nær-dublet, som
  kvalitetsgaten i pluginnet selv ville flagge.
