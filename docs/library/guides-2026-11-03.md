### FAQ

**What is the minimum for a men's steampunk costume?**
Three pieces: goggles at $24.95, a top hat at $34.95 and a coat at $39.95 — $99.85. Everything else is an
upgrade, not a requirement.

**Do I need a waistcoat?**
No. The coat does the work a waistcoat does, and a waistcoat under a split coat adds bulk where the silhouette
needs to stay long.

**Can I wear this outside a convention?**
Yes, and that is the point of the print pieces. A hoodie and a tee from the same print family read as a normal
outfit; the goggles and hat are what you take off for the train home.

**Which piece should I buy first?**
Goggles. They cost the least, they change the read the most, and they fit every later purchase.

**Is a top hat too much?**
Only if you wear it indoors. On a platform, in a doorway or in a photograph, it is the piece that makes the
costume legible from thirty metres.

**What sizes do the garments come in?**
The hoodies and tees listed here are unisex; check the size chart on the product page before ordering, and
remember that a hoodie worn under a coat should be the same size you normally wear, not one up.

**Where do I start if I want the whole shop?**
[Start here](https://steamgears.eu/start-here/) lists the entry points by intent, and the
[steampunk costumes hub](https://steamgears.eu/steampunk-costumes/) groups the capsules.

Yes, for goggles and a print — $46.85 in this shop. It is enough to photograph and to decide whether you like
the look; it is not enough to walk into a convention and be recognised as a character.

**Why does the second tier cost more than double the first?**
Because it adds the two pieces that carry the silhouette: a top hat at $34.95 and a garment at $26.95. The
first tier is two accessories; the second is an outfit.

**Which single piece gives the most costume per dollar?**
Goggles at $24.95. They are the only item no modern outfit contains, so they change the read of clothes you
already own.

**Can I mix the tiers?**
That is the sensible way to do it: buy the $50 tier, wear it once, then add the coat rather than the hat if you
live somewhere cold.

**Do prices include shipping?**
No. Shipping is calculated at checkout before you confirm the order, so the totals above are the clothing only.

**Will these pieces work after Halloween?**
The hoodies and tees do; the goggles and hat are the costume part. That split is deliberate in the tiers: the
more you spend, the more of the outfit stays in the wardrobe after the event.

**Where do the prices come from?**
From our own product pages on 2 November 2026, in USD. If a price has moved since, the product page wins.
