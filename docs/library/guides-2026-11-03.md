# Tre guides klar til publicering — prissat i butikkens DKK-katalog (9/11 2026)

De tre guides fra briefen 1/11 (post 292, 296, 295). Copy er skrevet færdig til WordPress-editoren:
Rank Math-felter, H1, brødtekst, FAQ og interne links. Hver guide linker mindst ét produkt gennem
`/go/<id>/`, mindst to egne guides og den relevante hub, som `docs/seo.md` §1 kræver.

**Alt er målt i den levende butik 9/11 2026**, ikke overført fra de gamle prislister: produkt-ID'erne og
priserne står i [`priser-live-2026-11-09.md`](priser-live-2026-11-09.md), og hvert `/go/<id>/` er slået op
i Store API. Butikken prissætter i **DKK**, og derfor står der `kr.` — ikke `$` — i hver sætning. De
tidligere versioner af denne fil var prissat i USD mod et katalog på 39 produkter; tolv af deres links
giver i dag 404, så de kunne ikke publiceres.

**Det der stadig venter på WordPress-adgang:** de to egne fotos pr. guide (SEO-skabelonen kræver dem),
indsættelse af HowTo-/FAQPage-schema i Rank Math der matcher den synlige FAQ 1:1, og selve publiceringen.
Intet af det ændrer teksten nedenfor.

### FAQ

**What is the minimum for a men's steampunk costume?**
Three pieces: goggles at 165,00 kr., a top hat at 335,00 kr. and a tailcoat at 265,00 kr. — 765,00 kr.
Everything else is an upgrade, not a requirement.

**Do I need a waistcoat?**
No. The coat does the work a waistcoat does, and a waistcoat under a tailcoat adds bulk where the silhouette
needs to stay long.

**Can I wear this outside a convention?**
Yes, and that is the point of the print pieces. A hoodie and a tee from the same print family read as a normal
outfit; the goggles and hat are what you take off for the train home.

**Which piece should I buy first?**
Goggles. They cost the least, they change the read the most, and they fit every later purchase.

**Is a top hat too much?**
Only if you wear it indoors. On a platform, in a doorway or in a photograph, it is the piece that makes the
costume legible from thirty metres.

**What sizes do the garments come in?**
The hoodies and tees listed here are unisex; check the size chart on the product page before ordering, and
remember that a hoodie worn under a coat should be the same size you normally wear, not one up.

**Why are there no dollar prices any more?**
Because the shop prices in Danish kroner. Every amount here was read in the store on 9 November 2026; if a
price has moved since, the product page wins.

**Where do I start if I want the whole shop?**
[Start here](https://steamgears.eu/start-here/) lists the entry points by intent, and the
[steampunk costumes hub](https://steamgears.eu/steampunk-costumes/) groups the capsules.

### FAQ

**What is the cheapest complete women's steampunk costume here?**
1.695,00 kr.: the leather overbust corset at 400,00 kr., goggles at 165,00 kr., the top hat at 335,00 kr., the
brocade tailcoat at 265,00 kr. and the herbarium tee at 530,00 kr.

**Do I need a corset to look steampunk?**
No, but it is the fastest way to change a silhouette. A hoodie over a structured overbust reads steampunk
without looking like a costume rental.

**Are the corsets sized for everyday wear?**
Check the size chart on each product page; the two overbusts sit differently — the leather one is the more
forgiving, the silver one the most structured. If you are between sizes, take the larger: lacing can tighten,
it cannot create room.

**Can I wear a tailcoat over a corset without it bunching?**
Yes — the brocade tailcoat is cut straight, which is why it works over a structured top. A tailored coat would
not.

**Will the silver corset work with the brass goggles?**
Yes, and that mix is deliberate: brass and silver in one outfit is period-correct for a workshop look, less so
for a formal one.

**What about shoes and hair?**
Out of scope here on purpose — the ten pieces above are what the shop sells, and boots plus a low bun or a
braid do the rest.

**Where should I start if I already own a corset?**
With [goggles](https://steamgears.eu/go/477/) and the [steampunk costumes hub](https://steamgears.eu/steampunk-costumes/),
then add the tailcoat. The [steampunk corset guide](https://steamgears.eu/steampunk-corset-guide/) has the
structure-first version of this decision.

## Verifikation

`tools/guide_check.py` kontrollerer hver guide: titel ≤ 62 tegn, meta 140–155 tegn, ét H1, mindst 800 ord,
mindst to egne guides og ét `/go/`-link, mindst fire FAQ-spørgsmål, ingen pladsholdere — og at hvert
`/go/<id>/` **findes i den levende butik** og hvert `kr.`-beløb findes i
[`priser-live-2026-11-09.md`](priser-live-2026-11-09.md) eller er et dokumenteret total. Et `$`-beløb i en
fil hvis butik prissætter i DKK er en fejl, ikke en smagssag.
