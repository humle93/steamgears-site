Two-minute styling rules

  • Goggles on the forehead, never on the eyes — unless you want to be a ww1 pilot for the night.
  • Mix exactly one metallic accent with matte fabrics; three brass items beat ten.
  • Corset over tee, not over bare skin — it reads more "inventor's workshop", and you will be warmer.
  • Boots anchor everything — any brown or black boot you own does the job.
