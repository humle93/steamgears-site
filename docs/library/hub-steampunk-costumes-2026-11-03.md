**H1:** Steampunk Costumes: Start With One Piece, Build the Rest

Most people build a steampunk costume backwards: they buy the biggest, cheapest bundle first, wear it once, and
find that nothing fits together. It works better the other way. One piece that changes how you are seen, then the
silhouette around it, then the layer you will still wear in February.

**Quick answer:** start with goggles (**$24.95**). They cost the least, they change the read the most, and they
fit every purchase you make afterwards. Add a top hat and a split coat and you have a complete costume for
**$99.85** — everything else on this page is an upgrade of that, not a requirement.

### FAQ

**What is the cheapest complete steampunk costume here?**
$99.85: goggles at $24.95, the top hat at $34.95 and the split coat at $39.95. It reads as a costume in a
photograph and as clothing on the way home.

**Do I need a corset?**
No. It is the fastest way to change a silhouette, not a requirement, and the brown lace-up is the version that
works over clothes for a whole day.

**What should I buy first if I am not sure?**
Goggles. They are $24.95, they work with every outfit on this page, and they are the piece people comment on.

**Can I wear this outside a convention?**
Yes — that is what the hoodie and tee are for. The printed layer reads as a normal outfit; the goggles and hat are
what you take off at the train.

**Which guide should I read first?**
[Men's essentials](https://steamgears.eu/mens-steampunk-costume-essentials/) or
[women's essentials](https://steamgears.eu/womens-steampunk-essentials/) depending on the silhouette you want;
both use the same ten-piece method, so you can switch later without starting over.

**How do I know the sizes?**
Each product page has its own size chart. Read that one rather than your usual clothing size, especially for the
corsets and the coats.

**What if the prices have moved?**
The prices here were read from our own product pages on 2 November 2026, in USD. The product page wins if they
differ.
