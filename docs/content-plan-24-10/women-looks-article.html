Three Rules That Make Any of These Work

  1. Layer in this order: base tee/blouse → corset → coat → hat. Never skip the middle layer.
  2. One metal family per outfit. Brass with brass, silver with silver.
  3. Goggles are a hat accessory first. On the brim 90% of the day, on the eyes for photos.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a steampunk outfit for women cost?

A real, complete steampunk outfit costs $52–200: around $52 for a tee-and-goggles kit, $75–90 for a first corset look, and $170–200 for a fully layered festival build. The table above itemises every tier.

Do I need a corset to dress steampunk?

No. A fitted vest, a belt worn high, or even a hoodie styled with brass accessories carries the look. Corsets simply read most "Victorian" in photos.

What do women wear under a steampunk corset?

A fitted t-shirt or high-collar blouse works best. Every look above is styled over a Steamgears tee, which keeps the lacing from sitting on skin.

Can I wear a steampunk hoodie to a convention?

Yes — pair it with a top hat or goggles and dark boots. Look 6 above is built exactly for that.
