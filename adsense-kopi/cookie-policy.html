What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. Some are necessary for the shop to work; others help us understand how the site is used.

Which cookies do we use?

  • Necessary: WooCommerce uses cookies to remember your cart and session. These cannot be disabled without breaking the shop.
  • Statistics: Google Analytics 4 (via Google Tag Manager) measures anonymised traffic so we can improve the site. Statistics cookies are only set after your consent.
  • Preferences: WordPress may store small preference cookies for logged-in users.

Your consent

When you visit steamgears.eu you can choose which cookie categories you allow. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by clearing cookies in your browser and revisiting the site.

Third parties

We use Google services for analytics. Google may transfer data outside the EU; safeguards are in place. See Google’s own privacy policy for details.
