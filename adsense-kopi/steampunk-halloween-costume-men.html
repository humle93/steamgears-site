Two-minute styling rules for men

  • Vest or coat over shirt, always — the structured layer is what separates steampunk from plain goth.
  • Goggles on the forehead or around the neck — on the eyes reads pilot cosplay, off the eyes reads steampunk gentleman.
  • One brass accessory is enough — pocket watch, goggles or belt. Three brass items beat ten.
  • Boots anchor everything — any brown or black boot you own does the job.
