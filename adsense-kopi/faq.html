Frequently Asked Questions

Updated 2026-11-04

Orders & payment

How do I place an order? Add items to your cart and check out. No account is required, but creating one lets you track your orders.

Which payment methods do you accept? We accept the payment methods shown at checkout. All payments are processed securely.

Production & shipping

How long does delivery take? Every printed product is made to order: production takes 3-7 business days, plus shipping time to your address. Gear items ship separately and may arrive in a separate package.

Where do you ship? We ship across the EU. Available countries are shown at checkout.

Can I track my order? Yes — you receive a shipping confirmation with tracking when your order is on its way.

Returns & exchanges

Can I return an item? Yes. EU customers have a 14-day right of withdrawal from the day you receive the item. Made-to-order printed items can be returned as long as they are unused and unwashed. Read the full policy on our Refund & Returns page.

What if the wrong size arrives? Contact us with your order number and a photo, and we will make it right.

Sizing & materials

Are the shirts true to size? The unisex fit is true to size. If you plan to layer over a waistcoat or hoodie-style look, consider one size up.

How do I care for printed garments? Wash inside out at 30°C and avoid tumble drying. The artwork stays crisp for years.

Contact

Still have a question? Use the contact page and we will reply as quickly as we can.
