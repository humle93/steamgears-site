Steampunk Couple Costumes: 10 Pairs Ready to Buy (2026)

Updated 2026-11-15

Couple costumes fail for one reason: one half looks like a costume and the other half looks like a person in a coat. The ten pairs below are built the other way round \u2014 each pair shares a silhouette, a palette or a single prop, so the two of you read as one idea in a photograph instead of two separate outfits.

How to pick a pair

Two rules cover most of it. Share one thing, not everything: matching coats in different colours reads better than identical outfits, and a shared prop \u2014 goggles, a pocket watch, a top hat \u2014 does the same job for a fraction of the spend. Let one half lead: decide who wears the bigger piece (a tailcoat, a corset, a hat) and keep the other half quiet but consistent, so the photograph has a centre.

1. The airship crew

Pilot and engineer. One half takes the wine red velvet tailcoat with copper pilot goggles; the other keeps it practical with the Airship Storm hoodie and the same goggles pushed up on the forehead. It reads as crew, not as two shoppers.

2. Inventor and patron

The time traveller top hat with goggles does the heavy lifting on one half; the other carries the Aeronaut brass pocket watch and a plain black shirt. Period detailing without a second costume.

3. Two corsets, one silhouette

The steampunk overbust corset and the gothic overbust corset are cut to the same line, so a pair can wear them over black without looking like a matching set bought in a bag.

4. Captain and first mate

Velvet tailcoat on one half, Victorian vampire tailcoat on the other, both in deep red. Same shape, two textures \u2014 the cheapest way to look intentional.

5. The clockwork pair

A Clockwork Seer pendant and the Aeronaut pocket watch tie the two outfits together in brass. Everything else can be ordinary clothing.

6. Moth and engraving

The Brass Luna Moth tee on one half and the Clockwork Rose art print as a carried prop or a framed photo backdrop \u2014 a quiet pair for indoor events and markets.

7. Navigator and cartographer

The Astrolabe Planisphere tee with a long coat on one half, and the Airship Armada tee on the other. Two prints, one subject: the sky you are both pretending to sail.

8. Brass menagerie

The Brass Fox Automaton tee and the Clockwork Octopus hoodie \u2014 an animal-either-side pair that works because both prints come from the same engraving style.

9. Explorer and archivist

Copper pilot goggles and a leather satchel on one half; the top hat with goggles and the Raven Chronometer art print carried as a “field drawing” on the other. Reads as an expedition with a story.

10. The understated pair

For a party where full costume is too much: the Airship Storm hoodie and the Brass Luna Moth tee, with the Clockwork Seer pendant on one half only. Two pieces of brass and no explaining required.

What to check before you buy

Sizes on the coats and corsets are the pieces people get wrong: measure the chest and the waist rather than ordering the size you wear in a t-shirt, and leave room for a shirt underneath. Prints and jewellery ship in one size, so a second colour of the same piece is the safe way to build a pair the week before an event.
