Delivery & Shipping

Updated 2026-11-14

How it works

Most of our products are made to order: when you order, your item is produced especially for you. This means:

  • Production: 3-7 business days for printed items (t-shirts, hoodies)
  • Shipping time is added on top, depending on your country
  • Posters and other wall art are printed in Europe close to you, so they can arrive separately from apparel in the same order

Shipping times

Estimated total delivery time after order:

  • Denmark: 6-10 business days
  • Rest of EU: 7-12 business days

Exact shipping options and prices are shown at checkout.

Tracking

You receive an e-mail with tracking information as soon as your order ships.

Multiple items

When you order several items, they may arrive in separate packages from different production partners — you pay shipping only once.

Returns

EU customers have a 14-day right of withdrawal. See the Refund & Returns page for the full policy.

Questions?

Reach us through the contact page.
