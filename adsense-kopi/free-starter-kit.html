What’s inside

12-Point Essentials Checklist

Every layer of a believable steampunk look, from base shirt to goggles — with a “why it matters” note on each item.

02

Budget Planner

A fill-in worksheet that splits your starter budget across DIY, thrifted and bought pieces — built around a $100 target.

03

One-Weekend Action Plan

Saturday and Sunday mapped out step by step, so you finish with a wearable costume instead of a half-built pile.

Who it’s for: makers who keep saving inspiration but never start, and cosplayers who want a first look that reads steampunk without a costume-shop budget. Beginners either buy one random accessory and call it a look — or drown in 40 Pinterest boards and never start. The Starter Kit fixes both: a fixed list of essentials, a budget cap, and a weekend schedule.

How to use it

1. Download the PDF and pick your character (captain, inventor, explorer or clockwork rogue).
2. Run through the 12-point checklist with what’s already in your wardrobe.
3. Follow the weekend plan — and use the Steamgears guides below for any piece you’d rather build or buy.
