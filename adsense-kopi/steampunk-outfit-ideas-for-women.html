The Five-Piece Rule

Every look in this guide can be rebuilt from five pieces. If you own these, you can improvise any of the twelve without buying a costume:

  1. A corset or corset belt — the anchor of the outfit.
  2. One styled layer — tee, hoodie or tailcoat.
  3. Goggles — on your hat, around your neck, or pushed up on your head.
  4. One brass accessory — pocket watch, ring or coat buttons.
  5. Shoes you can actually walk in — boots beat heels at cons.

One corset carries at least three of these looks, so start there and add the layer that matches the character you want.

Three Rules That Make Any of These Work

  1. Layer in this order: base tee/blouse → corset → coat → hat. Never skip the middle layer.
  2. One metal family per outfit. Brass with brass, silver with silver.
  3. Goggles are a hat accessory first. On the brim 90% of the day, on the eyes for photos.

How We Tested These Looks (Steamgears Workshop, October 2026)

Three of the twelve looks above were built and photographed side by side in the Steamgears workshop in Aabenraa in October 2026: the Airship Captain, the Cosy Engineer and the $52 Thrift Kit. Each was priced item by item in US dollars and timed from clothes rail to camera-ready.

Workshop notes: the corset read best worn over a full-coverage shirt, not under it — lacing over fabric keeps the silhouette steampunk and the outfit wearable for a full day. The hoodie looks worked only with the sleeves pushed up; ribbed cuffs at the wrist pulled the whole look back to sportswear. What we would do differently: hem-check every thrifted skirt before leaving the store — two of our finds were two inches too long and ate an hour.

In one line: pick a silhouette piece first, then let one gear piece — goggles, watch or corset — do the steampunk signalling.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a steampunk outfit for women cost?

A real, complete steampunk outfit costs $52–200: around $52 for a tee-and-goggles kit, $75–90 for a first corset look, and $170–200 for a fully layered festival build. The table above itemises every tier.

Do I need a corset to dress steampunk?

No. A fitted vest, a belt worn high, or even a hoodie styled with brass accessories carries the look. Corsets simply read most “Victorian” in photos.

What do women wear under a steampunk corset?

A fitted t-shirt or high-collar blouse works best. Every look above is styled over a Steamgears tee, which keeps the lacing from sitting on skin.

Can I wear a steampunk hoodie to a convention?

Yes — pair it with a top hat or goggles and dark boots. Look 6 above is built exactly for that.
