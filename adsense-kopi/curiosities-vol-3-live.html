Curiosities Vol. 3 Is Live — 8 New Clockwork Tees

Updated 2026-11-15

The workshop never sleeps: Curiosities Vol. 3 has landed in the shop — eight brand-new clockwork designs, from a hummingbird automaton to the Clockwork Seer tarot card.

Quick answer: Curiosities Vol. 3 is the third Steamgears tee collection: eight new emblem-based clockwork designs (from a hummingbird automaton to the Clockwork Seer) printed on the shop’s standard premium tees, sizes S to 2XL, with EU delivery. Two more designs – the Kraken and the Tea Automaton – are coming shortly.

Each design is printed on the same premium Bella+Canvas 3001 cotton tees you know, in sizes S–2XL. Browse the new arrivals on the shop page.

The eight designs

  • Hummingbird Automaton — a brass hummingbird with clockwork wings, over a natural-history plate
  • Nautilus Voyage — the Nautilus in cross-section, drawn as an engineer’s plate
  • Airship Armada — a fleet of airships in vintage engraving style
  • Clockwork Fox — a fox with exposed gearwork, in copperplate line
  • Cabinet of Curiosities — a cabinet of specimens, jars and instruments
  • Ink and Steam Muse — a writer’s muse in ink and steam
  • Chrono-Wing Dragonfly — a dragonfly with watch-hand wings
  • The Clockwork Seer — the tarot card from the story, in brass and antique cream

Tees are $26.95 in sizes S–2XL. Care, production time (3–7 business days) and delivery (6–10 business days in Denmark, 7–12 in the rest of the EU) are on each product page.
