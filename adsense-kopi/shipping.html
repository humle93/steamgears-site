Shipping — Timelines, Tracking & Split Deliveries

Updated 2026-11-14

Most Steamgears pieces are made to order: nothing sits in a warehouse waiting, so your item is produced after you order it. That is why the timeline has two parts instead of one.

Production, then delivery

  • Production: your item is printed and finished — 3–7 business days for printed items (t-shirts, hoodies).
  • Delivery: the parcel travels to you — added on top, depending on your country.

Total delivery time after ordering:

  • Denmark: 6–10 business days
  • Rest of the EU: 7–12 business days

Posters and wall art are printed by a separate print partner in Europe, so an order with both a poster and a garment can arrive in two parcels. The exact shipping options and prices for your address are always shown at checkout before you pay.

Why your order may arrive in more than one parcel

When you order several items, they can be produced and shipped by different partners. That means two parcels and two tracking links — but you only pay shipping once.

Tracking

As soon as your parcel ships you get an e-mail with tracking information. If tracking has not updated for a few days, that is usually a handover between carriers rather than a lost parcel; write to us and we will chase it.

Before you order

Sizing is the most common reason a garment comes back, and it is the easiest to avoid: measure a piece you already own and compare it with the size guide.

The full policy

Delivery times, rates and the legal wording live on the Delivery & Shipping page. For returns and the 14-day right of withdrawal, see Returns and the Refund & Returns policy.

Questions

Write to us through the contact page — we reply within 2–3 business days.
