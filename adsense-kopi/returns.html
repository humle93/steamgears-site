Returns — Start a Return, Refunds & Faulty Items

Updated 2026-11-06

Something does not fit, or is not what you expected? Here is the shortest path from “I want to return this” to “the money is back”.

1. The 14-day right of withdrawal

If you order as a consumer in the EU, you can cancel within 14 days of receiving the goods — no reason needed. The step-by-step version, including the standard EU form, is on the Right of withdrawal page.

2. Tell us before you send anything

E-mail cpaleadsis@gmail.com with your order number and a clear statement that you want to cancel or return. We send you the return instructions and the correct return address, because items ship from different warehouses — a parcel sent to the wrong address can take weeks to find its way back.

Please do not send an item back before you have heard from us.

3. Pack it as it arrived

Items must be unused and in their original packaging. You are only liable for any loss of value caused by handling beyond what is necessary to check the item — trying a garment on is fine, wearing it to a party is not.

Who pays the return shipping?

You do, unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong thing. In that case we cover it.

When the refund arrives

We refund within 14 days of receiving your withdrawal notice, using the same payment method you paid with. The refund covers the purchase price and the standard shipping cost of the original order. We may hold the refund until the goods are back with us, or until you have sent proof that you returned them.

The one exception: hygiene

Clothing worn in direct contact with the skin — including corsets with intimate contact areas — cannot be returned once the hygiene seal is removed, unless the item is faulty. This is a hygiene rule, not a judgement call.

Faulty or wrong item

Contact us within a reasonable time with your order number and photos of the problem. We send a replacement or refund, and your statutory rights apply: Danish consumer law gives a 2-year complaint right on manufacturing defects.

Avoid the return entirely

Most returns are sizing. Compare a garment you already own with the size guide before ordering — and if you are between two sizes, write to us and we will tell you which one the piece is cut for.

The full policy

The legal wording is on the Refund & Returns policy page, and shipping times are on Shipping and Delivery & Shipping.

Questions

E-mail cpaleadsis@gmail.com or use the contact page. We reply within 2–3 business days.
